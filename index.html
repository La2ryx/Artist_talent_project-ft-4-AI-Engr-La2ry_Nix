About Me

Behind the Canvas

I am a visual artist based in Miami, FL. Over the past decade, my work has focused on translating emotional landscapes into vivid acrylic and mixed-media creations.

Through bold textures and dynamic compositions, my pieces invite viewers to slow down and find narrative meaning in abstract forms.

Career Highlights
