About the Artist

Elena Rostova is a modern abstract artist specializing in large-scale oil canvases and vibrant color theory. Drawing inspiration from urban landscapes and organic movement, her work explores the balance between structure and dynamic motion.

With over a decade of dedication to visual arts, her creations invite viewers to experience texture and emotional depth through layered brushwork and luminous hues.
