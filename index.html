The practice

Art for the in-between moments.

Alex Rivera is a Miami-based visual artist translating emotional landscapes into vivid acrylic and mixed-media creations. Each piece begins with an instinct and becomes a conversation between bold texture, light, and movement.

From private commissions to public installations, the studio partners with collectors, designers, and cultural spaces to make work that feels unmistakably personal.
